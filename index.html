A SMALL BEGINNING. A SHARED BELIEF.

Komorebi began with four people and one simple thought: taking care of your skin should feel comforting.

Inspired by sunlight passing through leaves, we imagined a Filipino skincare brand built on gentle, reliable, affordable care. Something you can make part of your life, one ordinary day at a time.

Because progress isn't always a dramatic before and after. Sometimes, it is simply feeling more at home in your own skin.

Slowly is still forward.
01 / MISSION

Care within reach.

To make gentle, reliable, affordable skincare a simple and worthwhile part of everyday routines.

02 / VISION

Room to grow.

To become a trusted Filipino skincare brand that people grow with, shaping a healthier view of beauty through patient care.
